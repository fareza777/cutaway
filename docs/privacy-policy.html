Cutaway: 3D Objects

Privacy policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Cutaway: 3D Objects is an educational 3D app for exploring how everyday objects work. The core library, progress, quiz scores, saved parts, language, and appearance settings are stored on your device.

Information stored on your device

Cutaway does not require an account and does not send your library activity, saved parts, or quiz progress to our servers. These settings are kept locally so the app can remember your experience.

Advertising

The free version may show an interstitial after a completed quiz or a meaningful 3D viewing session. We use Google Mobile Ads to deliver these ads. The app requests non-personalized ads, but Google’s SDK may process device, app, network, advertising, diagnostic, impression, and interaction information as described in Google’s policies.

Learn more in Google’s partner-site privacy information. You can permanently disable Cutaway’s interstitial ads through the one-time Remove Ads purchase.

Purchases

Remove Ads is processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The app stores a local entitlement after Google Play reports a completed purchase and can restore that entitlement from Google Play on a later launch.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use Cutaway for account profiling, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking. Information handled by Google’s advertising and billing services is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Children

Cutaway is an educational app and does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us through the project issue tracker below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features or third-party services change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated with any revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue at github.com/fareza777/cutaway/issues.
