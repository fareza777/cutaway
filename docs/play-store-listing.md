## Full description

Look inside the things you use every day.

Cutaway: 3D Objects is an interactive 3D learning app for curious minds. Explore a library of mechanical, electronic, anatomical, and everyday objects—then take them apart until the way they work becomes clear.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Explore a growing library of real-world 3D objects.
• Rotate, zoom, and inspect every angle.
• Explode assemblies to separate the layers.
• Peel away the outside and use X-ray or cross-section views.
• Tap individual parts to learn what each one does.
• Follow step-by-step stories from input to output.
• Run mechanisms and watch the moving parts work together.
• Test yourself with interactive quizzes.

MADE FOR CURIOUS MINDS

Whether you are learning STEM, studying anatomy, teaching a class, building something, or simply wondering what is inside, Cutaway turns complex systems into something you can explore with your hands.

The core 3D library, progress, and saved parts work offline. No account is required. Search the library to find an object by name or description. The free version contains a library banner and interstitial ads at natural breaks. A one-time Remove Ads upgrade removes both formats on this device; the current price is shown by Google Play.

Keep asking better questions. Start with one object and follow the parts all the way through.
