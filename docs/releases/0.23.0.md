# Cutaway 0.23.0 - Rewarded ads and transition interstitials

Android versionCode: **28**. Package: `com.cutaway.explorer`.

## Release scope

- Rewarded (opt-in): a 30-minute break from full-screen ads (library and
  settings), and one quiz hint per question. Rewards are granted only on
  `EARNED_REWARD`. Remove-ads buyers never see the ad offer; their hints
  are free. Unit: `ca-app-pub-6279186647593327/7551778147`.
- Interstitial: preloaded once the SDK initializes, reloaded after close and
  after failures (15 s to 5 min backoff). Shown instantly, never awaited, on
  every 2nd natural transition: leaving a viewer session of at least 10 s, or
  leaving a finished quiz. 75 s client cooldown, 60 s launch grace period,
  12 per session ceiling; AdMob cap is 3 per 10 minutes. The quiz-result
  trigger was removed.
- Banner: anchored adaptive banner also on the saved screen.
- Non-personalized ad requests and the UMP consent flow are unchanged.
